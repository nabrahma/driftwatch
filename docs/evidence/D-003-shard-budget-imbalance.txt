Evidence for docs/DISCOVERIES.md D-003.

Claim: enforcing a global key budget as per-shard budgets loses capacity
to hash imbalance. The overflow column is the number of keys that hash to
an over-subscribed shard and are evicted while other shards sit under
their share, i.e. capacity paid for and not used.

Keys are "block-<n>" for n in [0, keys), hashed with xxhash.Sum64String,
which is exactly what pkg/oracle does.

Confirmed end to end: seeding an oracle configured with
MaxTrackedKeys=1,000,000 and the default 64 shards with exactly 1,000,000
distinct keys leaves 996,885 tracked - 3,115 evicted, matching the
predicted overflow for that configuration exactly.

## Direction 1 — libzmq PUB to Go SUB

10,000 messages across three topics, alternating between the two framing
conventions, each carrying a binary payload with a null byte, a newline, high
bytes and a UTF-8 sequence in it.

6,667 arrived, which is exactly right. The subscription is to `kv-events`, ZMQ
subscription filtering is a prefix match, and two of the three topics
(`kv-events` and `kv-events-secondary`) carry that prefix. The test asserts the
exact set of sequence numbers rather than the count, so a filter that dropped
the right number of the wrong messages would fail.

Both framing conventions parsed. Every binary payload arrived byte-identical.

## Direction 2 — Go PUB to libzmq SUB

5,000 messages, one topic, no filtering. All 5,000 arrived, contiguous, with
binary payloads intact.

## What this proves

ADR-0001 chose a pure-Go ZMTP implementation over a cgo binding to libzmq, which
buys static binaries and a distroless image and costs a guarantee: wire
compatibility becomes a claim rather than something the linker enforces. These
two tests are what makes it a measurement instead.

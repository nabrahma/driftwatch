D-012 — the publisher label limit and the cardinality budget from §9 M12 are
        mutually incompatible at the values the PRD gives.

Measured by driving the ingest path with 10,000 keys and 500 distinct
publishers, at four values of maxPublisherLabels, and counting the time series
the registry would expose (histogram buckets counted individually, plus _sum,
_count and the implicit +Inf).

    $ go test -run TestEvidence_D012 -v ./pkg/metrics/

    === RUN   TestEvidence_D012
        maxPublisherLabels=25   -> 156 time series (budget 500) OK
        maxPublisherLabels=50   -> 306 time series (budget 500) OK
        maxPublisherLabels=75   -> 456 time series (budget 500) OK
        maxPublisherLabels=100  -> 606 time series (budget 500) OVER BUDGET
    --- PASS: TestEvidence_D012 (0.07s)

The arithmetic behind it: §12 defines seven metrics carrying the `publisher`
label —

    driftwatch_events_received_total
    driftwatch_events_dropped_total
    driftwatch_seq_gaps_total
    driftwatch_seq_missing_events
    driftwatch_publisher_restarts_total
    driftwatch_publisher_clock_skew_seconds
    driftwatch_gapset_truncated

Six of the seven are touched by a plain ingest workload, and each costs
(limit + 1) series once the __other__ aggregate is included. At the PRD's
default of 100 that is 6 x 101 = 606 before any other metric is counted, which
is 21% over the whole budget the same section sets.

The default is therefore 50 rather than 100. See docs/DECISIONS.md ADR-0008.

Standing guard, so the two numbers cannot silently diverge again:

    $ go test -run Cardinality -v ./pkg/metrics/

    === RUN   TestMetrics_CardinalityStaysBoundedUnderTenThousandKeys
        10000 keys and 500 publishers produced 329 time series (budget 500,
        50 publisher labels admitted)
    --- PASS: TestMetrics_CardinalityStaysBoundedUnderTenThousandKeys (0.01s)
    === RUN   TestMetrics_CardinalityStaysBoundedWithEveryMetricExercised
        every metric exercised with 500 publishers: 597 time series (budget 700)
    --- PASS: TestMetrics_CardinalityStaysBoundedWithEveryMetricExercised (0.00s)

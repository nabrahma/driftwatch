D-029: the extras scan publishes target health it never measured

Captured from the e2e E1 diagnostics bundle, run of 2026-08-02, and from the
regression test that reproduces it.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. The symptom, from 03-manager.log in
   E1-HappyPath-reports-the-conditions-an-operator-gates-on

request for the whole run. Only the DEBUG event lines are shown.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. The cause
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

pkg/sweeper/sweeper.go — TargetHealth is assigned in exactly two places, both
inside the oracle-to-target sweep:

    379:  rep.TargetHealth = after
    381:  rep.TargetHealth = health

pkg/sweeper/extras.go builds its own report and sets only the pass:

    62:   rep.Pass = differ.PassTargetToOracle

differ.Report.TargetHealth is a struct, so on an extras report it is the zero
value, and the zero value of Reachable is false. Both consumers took it at
face value:

    pkg/check/check.go:1376  c.targetUnreachable.Store(!rep.TargetHealth.Reachable)
    pkg/check/check.go:1324  c.m.SetTargetReachable(rep.TargetHealth.Reachable)
    pkg/check/check.go:1325  c.m.SetTargetKeyspaceSize(rep.TargetHealth.KeyspaceSize)

The first drives the CRD status and the phase. The second and third drive the
gauges an alert fires on. All three were reporting a measurement nobody took.

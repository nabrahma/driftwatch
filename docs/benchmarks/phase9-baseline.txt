driftwatch Phase 9 benchmark baseline
=====================================

Six repetitions per benchmark (`make bench BENCHCOUNT=6`), which is what
benchstat needs to put a confidence interval on a difference. A baseline with
one sample per benchmark cannot be compared against: benchstat reports "~" for
every row and the gate passes whatever the numbers say.

Supersedes the phase1 and phase2 baselines, which predate Go 1.25, the msgpack
and template codecs, and the batch-read change in pkg/target that removed a
redundant copy of every Read (see G-004).

BenchmarkFullSweep1M and BenchmarkGetMany500Real are not here. Both need a real
Redis and run under the integration tag; `make bench-sweep` captures the first,
and G-004 records the second.
